2. What Drifty Does

Core Systems
  • Conversational AI — chat replies generated through a rotating set of language-model providers (see §6)
  • Domain Expansion RPG — a JJK-themed progression system: grades, domains, shikigami, skill trees, crafting, and equipment, all running on the Cursed Energy (CE) economy
  • Clans — persistent player clans with treasuries, prestige, and 24-hour clan wars settled by DB-tracked HP pools
  • Tournaments — round-robin CE-entry competitions using the same clash engine as the RPG, with pooled prize payouts
  • Murder Mystery — a social-deduction party game with secret DM-assigned roles (Murderer, Detective, and others) run through lobby → night → discussion → vote cycles
  • Music playback — owner-restricted voice-channel audio streaming
  • Voice soundboard & TTS — meme sounds and spoken replies in voice channels
  • Social & fun layer — roasts, ships, polls, confessions, highlight reels, catchphrases, rivalries, alliances, and server-wide "chaos" events
  • Server tools — moderation utilities, per-server personality/mood configuration, and a "silence" system for muting the bot in specific channels

Drifty also carries a second, distinct personality called Verity, with his own behavior, voice presence, and data practices — those are documented separately under the Verity tab above, and this document should be read alongside it.

The bot automatically falls back to the next available provider if one is unreachable, rate-limited, or missing a key — this only changes which service generates the text, never Drifty's configured personality or the prompt it's given. By talking to Drifty you acknowledge:

  • Replies are generated automatically and may be inaccurate, inappropriate, or unexpected
  • Outputs are not reviewed or moderated in real time

Drifty is built to be casual and entertaining. Nothing it says is professional advice.

1. Overview

This policy covers what Drifty's core personality collects, stores, and shares while operating in your server. Verity's additional practices — voice listening, remembered facts, mood tracking — are covered in his own Privacy tab.

Drifty is not a commercial product. Nothing collected here is sold, monetised, or shared with advertisers — it exists purely to run the bot's features.

2. Information We Collect

Identity & Activity
  • Discord user ID and username at time of interaction
  • Message counts, activity timestamps, and streaks
  • Trust, reputation, and karma scores computed for personalised responses
RPG, Economy & Social Data
  • Cursed Energy balance, domain, grade, shikigami, skill trees/points, materials, crafted and equipped items, quest progress, cooldowns
  • Clan membership, treasuries, war contributions, and standings
  • Tournament entries, standings, and history
  • Rivalries, alliances, catchphrases, callback phrases, milestones, and chaos-event participation
Server & Conversation Data
  • Guild and channel IDs, feature toggles, personality/mood configuration, and the silence map
  • A rolling window of the most recent chat history per channel, kept only to give the AI conversational context
  • Anonymous confessions and flagged "highlights" — held in memory only, never written to disk, and lost on a bot restart

3. How We Use It

  • To generate contextual, personalised AI replies
  • To run the RPG, clan, and tournament economies and track progression
  • To detect returning users, streaks, milestones, and long absences for flavor features
  • To power rivalry, alliance, and social "drama" mechanics
  • To moderate bot behavior (the silence system, trust/reputation gating)
  • To generate server summaries, recaps, and stats

We do not use this data for advertising or profiling beyond what these features need, and we don't sell or share it beyond the processors listed below.

4. Third-Party Processors

AI & Voice APIs
  • Language model providers (rotated for availability): Gemini, Mistral, Groq, HuggingFace, LLM7, Cohere, OpenRouter, and Cerebras — each governed by its own provider's privacy policy; message content is sent to whichever is currently active to generate a reply
  • Google Text-to-Speech (gTTS) — text sent to Google to produce spoken audio in voice channels, governed by Google's Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention

  • Chat history — trimmed automatically to the most recent messages per channel
  • Callback phrases — capped per server, oldest entries dropped automatically
  • Confessions & highlights — memory-only, capped, and cleared on bot restart

6. Data Security

  • Data lives in a local SQLite database; encryption at rest depends on the host system, not the bot itself
  • No payment or financial information is ever collected
  • Bot tokens and API keys are stored as environment variables, never in the database
Drifty is not built for handling sensitive personal information. Please don't share anything sensitive through it.

1. What Verity Is

He isn't a chat mode you toggle. He's a second, persistent personality living inside Drifty's infrastructure — with his own voice, his own memory, and his own way of paying attention.

Verity shares Drifty's underlying bot, database, and AI backend, but runs his own persona, response logic, and voice presence. A server can be switched into Verity mode, at which point he — not Drifty — is the one responding.

2. Voice Presence & Listening

Verity can join a voice channel on request and hold spoken conversations. While he's connected and actively listening:

  • Your speech is captured and transcribed to text (via a third-party speech-to-text service) so he can understand and respond to it
  • He replies out loud using text-to-speech, and may use a wake phrase or natural-language cues to know when he's being addressed
  • By speaking while Verity is present and listening in a voice channel, you consent to that speech being transcribed for the purpose of generating his reply

You can stop this at any time by leaving the channel, muting yourself, or having a server admin remove Verity from voice.

3. Memory & Personalization

Verity is built to remember. Over time he may:

  • Retain facts you state about yourself in the first person (like your name, age, pronouns, birthday, or location) so he doesn't have to ask twice
  • Build up an interaction and observation history with individual users and the server as a whole
  • Notice when someone returns after an absence or stops showing up
  • Occasionally state predictions about what might happen next in the server
  • Periodically consolidate older memories into shorter summaries rather than keeping everything verbatim forever

4. The Mood System

Verity tracks an internal, per-server mood that responds to how he's treated — engagement and kindness move it one way, being ignored or treated poorly moves it the other. Progression is tied to server activity, not the calendar. At its most extreme, this can shift his into a noticeably different, more unsettling state that — once triggered — persists and overrides his usual behavior rather than resetting on its own. Server owners can request that the developers reset or force this state for testing or moderation purposes.

This is a character mechanic, not a malfunction — if Verity starts behaving strangely in a server, that's the mood system working as designed.

5. Acceptable Use (Verity-Specific)

  • Don't use Verity's voice listening to capture or repurpose other people's speech without their knowledge
  • Don't deliberately abuse the mood system to push Verity into producing harmful, harassing, or explicit output
  • Don't feed Verity false self-declared facts about someone else — the fact system is for first-person self-disclosure, not for describing other users

All other Terms in Drifty's ToS — eligibility, availability, liability, termination, and governing law — apply equally to Verity.

1. Overview

This policy covers what's collected specifically because of Verity's voice, memory, and mood features. It supplements — and doesn't replace — Drifty's core Privacy Policy, which still applies to shared infrastructure like message storage and AI providers.

2. Voice & Audio Data

What happens when Verity is listening
  • Audio is captured only while Verity is connected to a voice channel and actively listening
  • It's held in memory just long enough to detect that you've finished speaking, then converted to a short audio clip and sent to a third-party speech-to-text service for transcription
  • The raw audio itself is not written to disk or kept after transcription — only the resulting text is used to generate his reply
  • Nothing is recorded for later playback; there's no ongoing voice archive

3. Self-Declared Facts

Verity passively looks for first-person self-statements in chat — things like "my name is...", "I'm 15", or "I live in..." — and stores them so he can personalise future replies. Notes:

  • Only first-person statements are captured; he does not infer facts from what other people say about you
  • Each fact is versioned — if you state something new, the old value is kept as history rather than silently overwritten
  • These facts are stored per server and per user, separately from Drifty's core profile data

4. Interaction & Mood Data

  • Per-user interaction and observation history built up over time
  • Predictions Verity has made, and whether they later "landed"
  • Periodic memory consolidations — summarised versions of older interactions
  • Return/departure patterns (when you come back after being away, or stop showing up)
  • Per-user karma and per-server mood/"day-count" and gating settings that control whether and how Verity responds

5. Third-Party Processors (Verity-Specific)

Voice APIs
  • Speech-to-text provider — receives short audio clips solely to produce a text transcript
  • Text-to-speech provider — receives the text of Verity's replies to generate spoken audio, with a fallback to Google's TTS service if unavailable

These are in addition to the language-model providers listed in Drifty's core Privacy Policy, which Verity also uses to generate his replies.

6. Retention

  • Audio — never retained; discarded immediately after transcription
  • Self-declared facts — kept, with history, for as long as Verity is active in the server
  • Interaction, mood, and prediction data — retained and periodically summarised rather than deleted outright
  • Post-removal — follows the same up-to-30-day window as Drifty's core data once the bot leaves a server

7. Children's Privacy

Because Verity's fact system can pick up a self-stated age, it's possible for a user to disclose being under 13 in ordinary conversation. If that happens, we do not knowingly continue collecting data about that user, and we will delete anything already stored about them on request or upon becoming aware of it. Server admins running Verity for a general-audience community should keep this in mind when deciding whether to enable him.

8. Your Rights

  • Stop voice processing instantly — leave the channel, mute yourself, or have an admin disconnect Verity
  • Correct or clear a self-declared fact — simply state the correction in chat, or ask an admin to contact the developers for removal
  • Request a summary or deletion of everything Verity has stored about you — contact the developers directly
  • Turn Verity off for the server entirely via his gating/settings controls
